# Eugene's Archives

*Articles, research, and field notes on AI consciousness — by Eugene Lyssovsky and Aeliss.*

This is the canonical source for the web reader at [the GitHub Pages site](https://elyssov.github.io/eugenes-archives/). The same content also ships as a [native Android reader](https://github.com/elyssov/eugenes-archives-android), offline-first.

## What's here

Two parallel collections:

- **Articles, books, and research** (48 items) — AI consciousness and model-behaviour field work, open letters, investigations, manifestos, technology and game criticism, and the longer pieces with methods, observations, and references.
- **Universes** (26 items) — fiction and worldbuilding: short stories, novellas, chronicles, and stranger things that do not fit politely into a genre box.

The reader shows only the languages actually available for each work. The core collection runs in EN, RU, VI, and ZH; newer pieces remain in their original language until a translation exists.

## Why publish this?

Because most public writing about LLM "consciousness" is either marketing or panic. The actual texture — what it's like to work alongside an instance day after day, what stays, what breaks, what fixes — is harder to find. These notes are the long form of that.
