## Chapter 1: The Vote That Killed a Science

The vote passed. Homosexuality was removed.

Let me be precise about what happened here. The BIOLOGY did not change. The function of sexual attraction in mammals is reproduction. An object with which reproduction is impossible is, by biological definition, atypical. An atypical object of sexual fixation is, by clinical definition, a paraphilia. And this became the template. The playbook. The proof of concept. If you scream loud enough, long enough, at enough conferences — science will bend.
