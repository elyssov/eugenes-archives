Chapter 4: The Gender Factory

And yet, in 2024, between 1.5% and 2% of American teenagers identified as "transgender." A fifteen-hundred-fold increase over the biological baseline. In a single generation.

This is not an epidemic of gender dysphoria. This is an epidemic of DIAGNOSIS. Or more precisely — an epidemic of sociogenic vectorization of pre-existing mental illness into a culturally approved form.
