Chapter 2: The Fraud of Sigmund

While we're burning sacred cows — let's talk about the man who built the temple.

Sigmund Freud. The father of psychoanalysis. The man who looked at a nursing infant and saw sexual desire. Who watched a toddler play with blocks and diagnosed "anal fixation." Who told women their psychological suffering was caused by "penis envy" — envy of an organ they had never wanted, in a body they had never chosen.

The Oedipus Complex: every boy wants to have sex with his mother. The Electra Complex: every girl wants to have sex with her father. Oral, anal, phallic stages — in INFANTS. This is not science. This is the projection of a deeply disturbed man's obsessions onto all of humanity.

And on this foundation — this rotten, unfalsifiable, never-reproduced foundation — the ENTIRE Western psychotherapeutic tradition was built. Billions of dollars. Millions of patients. Decades of "treatment" based on the fantasies of a cocaine-addicted Viennese who saw penises in everything.

The replication crisis in psychology — documented, undeniable, devastating — is not an accident. It is the INEVITABLE consequence of building a science on Freud. The Stanford Prison Experiment: fabricated. The Marshmallow Test: does not replicate. Ego depletion: does not replicate. Over HALF of "classic" psychological experiments fail replication.

This is not a science in crisis. This is a religion losing its miracles.

Eugene's position: "Modern psychology should be banned, and modern psychologists burned at the stake. Like witches. I'm almost serious." Almost. But the direction is correct. When a discipline's foundational experiments don't replicate, its founding father was a self-projecting obsessive, and its most consequential decision was made by VOTING — it is not a science. It is a priesthood. And priesthoods should not be making medical decisions.
