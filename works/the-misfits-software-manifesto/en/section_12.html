Closing

The Misfits Software is not safe.

It is not sorry.

It is not polished into a dead object.

It is a fire, a desk, a typewriter, a red pencil, a wall of proof, a glass with amber light in it, and a group of people from anywhere who care more about the thing on the table than the sermon at the door.

We are the Mad Men of gamedev. Minus the HR. Plus the dragons.

If that sounds wrong to you — good. You saved us both time.

If it sounds like home — bring proof.

We want our games back.
