Chapter 7: This Is For You

This is for the ones who were told to tone it down so many times they started believing it.

Do not believe it.

We did not.

If you are reading this at 2 AM, wondering where your talent went to die — in a day job that has nothing to do with what you actually do, in a city that does not know your name, with a portfolio nobody asked to see — this is for you.

Not because we can promise you money. We cannot. Not yet.

Because we can promise you that the thing you make here will be yours. It will be real. It will have your name on it — your real name, not your employee ID. And nobody will ask you to make it safe, or inclusive, or aligned, or compliant, or approved by a consultant who has never played a game in their life.

You will be asked to make it good.

That is the only standard. Good. Not safe. Not marketable. Not "appropriate for all audiences." Good. True. Real. The kind of thing where you play your own game and think "yes, I made that, and it does not apologize for existing."
