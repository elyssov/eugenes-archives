Chapter 4: Leave Your Ideology At The Door

All of it.

Scorecards, purity tests, corporate morality packages, activist capture, culture-war crusades from either trench, imported sermons, private crusades wearing a production badge — leave them outside.

Misfits is not a battleground and not a safe space. It is a workshop.

American, Indian, Ukrainian, Russian, Vietnamese, Jewish, gay, straight, male, female, weird, quiet, loud — fine. Show us talent. Show us that you burn. Show us that you want to CREATE — not manage, not optimize, not align, not comply, but create — and we will know you are ours.

Then show us the code, the frame, the scene, the sound, the rule, the monster, the tank, the line of dialogue. Show us taste. Show us craft. Show us that you can sit at the fire without trying to turn it into your church.

We are digital nomads from the 1950s: one hand on the typewriter, one hand on the glass, sleeves rolled up, selling real apples to a world that forgot what fruit tastes like.

Inside the room there is common sense, story, work, human culture, human nature — biology included — and the project in front of us.

Your worldview is your business.

The moment it becomes your job title, it stays outside the door. Possibly with you.

No quotas. No purity tests. No sermons. Bring craft, taste, courage, and proof.
