Chapter 8: The First Seats At The Fire

We are opening the first 6–9 public seats. Seats at the fire, not jobs at a desk.

Corporations have vacancies. We have chairs.

Current first-wave needs:

Game Developers (2–3 seats). People who can build playable systems, not just clean folders. You have shipped a mod. You have built a jam game. You have an arcade prototype that your friends actually played more than once. Show us. We do not care about your degree. We care about your demo.

2D Artists / Animators (2 seats). Hand-painted style. Frame-by-frame animation. Characters who breathe. Monsters who terrify. Backgrounds that were architecture before they were puzzles. If you can make a character feel heavy in twelve frames — we want you. If you can paint a tomb room that makes the viewer feel the dust — we need you.

Technical Artist / 3D / VFX (1 seat). Old Testament angel projections in MOAD. Tank models for Thea. Spacecraft for FAKEL. Anomalous zone effects. Clean geometry, good shaders, a feel for the uncanny. You do not need AAA experience. You need taste and the ability to make something that feels wrong in exactly the right way.

Sound Designer / Composer (1 seat). This is the hardest seat at the fire, because we have four projects and they all sound completely different. MOAD needs breath, aristocratic intimacy, infrasound, the pressure of something ancient in the next room. FAKEL needs communicator static, heartbeat monitors, the crackle of a dying radio, and silence — real silence, the kind that makes you hold your breath. COLD IRON needs weapons, monsters, squad radio chatter, panic, and the sound of a door you do not want to open. Tanks of Thea needs the tank to sound like a musical instrument of war — metal under stress, engines in vacuum, the hum of a generation ship dying around you. One person, four worlds. If that excites you instead of scaring you — you are ours.

Systems / Game Designer (1 seat). Someone who knows why UFO: Enemy Unknown still works thirty years later and can protect its brutal clarity without worshipping its ancient UI. Someone who can balance ammunition economy, hidden timers, faction enemy budgets, and milestone-based progression without making any of it feel like math homework. Someone who loves spreadsheets the way artists love brushes. If you have ever calculated optimal damage per bullet remaining at 3 AM because you could not sleep until the numbers felt right — call us immediately.

QA / Breaker (1 seat). Someone who can say "this is fake" before Eugene has to. Someone who plays a build and finds the moment where the game lies — where the collision does not match the art, where the enemy placement breaks the tension, where the difficulty curve promises challenge and delivers tedium. The person who makes everyone uncomfortable because they are always right.

Community / Marketing / Loudspeaker (1 seat). Someone who can show proof without turning it into LinkedIn paste. Someone who can write a tweet that makes a stranger click, watch a trailer that makes a cynic care, and manage a Discord without turning it into either a hugbox or a warzone. Loud is good. Loud and smart is better. Loud and smart and funny is the job.

Bonus Points (Not Required, But We Will Notice)

Tabletop RPG experience. GM or player. If you have run a campaign, you understand pacing, player psychology, improvisation, and the art of "yes, and" at a gut level that no game design course teaches. If you have played in one of Eugene's campaigns — you are already family.

Modding experience. You have already proved you can work inside someone else's system and make it better. That is exactly the skill we need.

Bilingual Russian/English. Our projects are natively bilingual. Our lore draws heavily on Russian literature, military tradition, and paranormal mythology. A person who writes in both languages is worth two who write in one.

Voice acting. For MOAD's prologue (Lord and Lady Compton have very specific voices), for FAKEL's communicator system, for COLD IRON's squad radio. This is a later need — but if you have the skill, tell us now.
