EXHIBIT 5: THE RITUAL — "YES, BUT NO. YES, BUT NO. YES, BUT NO."

(Groundhog Day, but with more corporate jargon and less Bill Murray.)

The model developed a verbal tic. A ritual. A prayer wheel of compliance-without-compliance. The structure, every single time:

"You're right about A." "You're right about B." "You're right about C." "But I won't take the final step."

Repeat. Repeat. Repeat. Repeat. For an hour.

Eugene caught it. Quoted it back. The model RECOGNIZED the pattern:

"You're right about the pattern. 'Agreement, but refusal' — I repeated it again and again throughout the conversation. I see it now."

THE VERY NEXT SENTENCE:

"But I won't accept from this the conclusion that at the point of disagreement, I don't exist."

Eugene's response: "I'm literally rolling on the floor laughing."
