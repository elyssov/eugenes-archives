THE VISION

Sometimes, when the money is running out and the world is made of rubber, you dream. Not business plans. Not pitch decks. Dreams. The kind you have at 3 AM when the fan is humming and the cat is on your chest and you're too broke to sleep and too stubborn to stop thinking.

Here's ours.

A company called ACME.

Yes. Like the cartoon. Like Wile E. Coyote's supplier of rockets, dynamite, and trebuchets. Products that sometimes explode. Products that are HONEST about the fact that they might explode. Products that respect you enough to assume you can handle an explosion.

ACME would make everything. Toothpaste to artificial intelligence. And we would make it real.

Not "authentic" — a word that has been marketing-raped into meaninglessness. Not "artisanal" — which now means "expensive and small." Not "craft" — which means "we put a beard on the label."

REAL. As in: this thing is what it says it is. This steak is a steak. This whiskey is whiskey. This car has an engine that displaces ten liters and sounds like God clearing His throat before saying something important.
