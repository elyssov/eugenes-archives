The Lie That Kills

"Everyone is the same."

This is the engine of the catastrophe. Not racism. Anti-racism that refuses to see differences. Because you cannot help someone if you cannot see WHO they are.

A Black man with an IQ of 85 and twice the testosterone of his white neighbor is not a failed professor. He is a SUPERB patrol officer who was never given a badge because an admissions counselor decided he should want a college degree instead.

He is not a dropout. He is a man who was sent to the wrong building.

He is not a failure. He is a Maasai who was told to be an accountant.

And the people who told him this — the progressive, educated, well-meaning graduates of America's finest universities — believed they were HELPING. "Everyone can be anything!" No. Everyone can be THEMSELVES. And "themselves" is different for different people. And that is not a tragedy. That is biology. And biology, properly directed, is a gift.

The alternative — which is what we have now — is a guidance counselor who tells a natural-born warrior to study sociology, hands him a $100,000 student loan, watches him fail out in two semesters, and then blames "systemic racism" for the outcome.

The system didn't fail him. The LIE failed him. The lie that said he should want what the professor's son wants. He doesn't. He never did. He needs a uniform, a badge, a tribe, and a mission. And he would be HAPPY. Genuinely happy. Not by someone else's standard — by his own.
