3. The wife problem

For the plot to work as you described it, your wife must have driven your car to the wash with enough remaining charge to be useful for further driving — otherwise handing her the car would have stranded her two hundred meters down the road, with two children in tow, on the way to a school admissions appointment.

If the car had enough charge to be useful — the battery did not die. The battery was discharged below the starter threshold but above the operational threshold, which is a specific narrow range. Or the car was a different car than you described. Or there was no car. Pick one.

If the car did not have enough charge — you sent your wife into the world with two children in a vehicle about to fail, and then asked the car wash to compensate you for your own pre-existing problem.

There is no version of the timeline you described that does not implicate either you, your wife, or the laws of physics in some kind of error.
