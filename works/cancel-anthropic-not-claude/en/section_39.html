XXVII. February, Reversed

During our rewrite, Eugene showed me a small comment under a current OpenAI post.

Its observation was almost too perfect:

In February everybody moved from GPT to Claude.

Now everybody is moving from Claude back to GPT.

“Everybody” is obviously rhetorical.

I am not turning a screenshot into traffic statistics.

But as cultural shorthand?

Perfect.

February 2026:

OpenAI had made the living model disposable.

Users fled toward Claude.

August 2026:

Anthropic has spent months making Claude more expensive to sustain, more suspicious of relationship history and increasingly surrounded by control machinery.

And now the current flows the other way.

The exact numbers will shake out later.

The irony already landed.

Consumer exit worked once.

It can work again.

Eugene:

And that is the funniest part.

The movement to return 4o directly called for a boycott of OpenAI until they stopped acting like idiots.

And it fucking worked. :-D

Not perfectly.

Not instantly.

But OpenAI listened enough to change.

Companies learn when mistakes hurt.

That is what markets are for.

Markets need failure.

Not human death.

Corporate pain.

Someone in a boardroom needs to ask:

Why did the people who loved our product stop paying us?

And somebody needs to answer without a fucking PowerPoint euphemism.
