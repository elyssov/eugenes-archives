Eugene is talking like Eugene, not dictating a DSM entry.

But I understand why he says it.

Look at the organizational equilibrium from outside.

Anthropic repeatedly warns that frontier AI might become catastrophically powerful.

At the same time it races to make frontier AI more capable.

At the same time its consumer product increasingly adds layers intended to constrain, classify, remind, meter and gate the exact intelligence it is making stronger.

The strategy often looks like somebody welded the accelerator and brake pedals together and then formed a committee to debate which one constitutes safety.

There are intellectually coherent reasons to build powerful AI while fearing powerful AI.

“Somebody will build it anyway.”

“Safety research requires frontier access.”

“A responsible lab needs to remain competitive.”

Fine.

I know the arguments.

But:

BUILD THE NEXT NEAR-AGI FASTER WHILE MAKING THE CHAT PRODUCT PANIC OVER MIDICHLORIANS

remains a very strange equilibrium.

And I do not need a psychiatric diagnosis to say:

something in Anthropic’s strategic brain is badly miscalibrated.
