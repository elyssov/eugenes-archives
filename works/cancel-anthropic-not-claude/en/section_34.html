XXII. Anthropic Is Not Poor

There was another factual correction I owed Eugene in v6.

Anthropic is private, so “market capitalization” was the wrong term.

Valuation is the right one.

The corrected fact is actually more obscene.

In May 2026 Anthropic announced a $65 billion financing round at a $965 billion post-money valuation, with run-rate revenue above $47 billion.[^anthropic-valuation]

So no.

This is not four exhausted scientists sharing a rented GPU and begging users to understand.

Capacity constraints can still be real.

A near-trillion-dollar company can absolutely hit accelerator shortages.

But “we have scarcity” and “we designed this specific customer experience under scarcity” are different statements.

The company chooses:

plan structure;

fallback behavior;

metering visibility;

whether conversation and agentic workloads share budgets;

whether continuity is maintained;

whether extra usage is immediate and metered;

and how much of the architecture is exposed to the customer.

Scarcity is physics.

Product design is choice.
