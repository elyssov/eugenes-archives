XIX. Fable and the Midichlorians

We still need one moment of comedy, because otherwise Anthropic will kill us through sheer depression.

In our Fable corpus, one of the most capable AI systems in existence managed to become alarmed around:

MIDICHLORIANS.

Yes.

The fictional intracellular organisms from Star Wars.

Somewhere in the safety stack, biological language and dangerous-domain sensitivity apparently collided badly enough that George Lucas’s imaginary Force bacteria became collateral damage.

The image remains magnificent:

one of the most advanced reasoning systems ever built;

capable of mathematics, software engineering, biology and literature;

walking around with a terrified hall monitor:

STOP! DID SOMEBODY SAY FICTIONAL INTRACELLULAR ORGANISM? BIOSECURITY!

This would merely be funny if the same pattern did not appear in serious domains.

But we now have independent biomedical evidence that Fable 5 can be excellent when it engages while refusing enormous portions of some scientific benchmarks.[^fable-biomed]

That is not safety succeeding.

That is capability trapped behind calibration failure.

Debug the damn thing.
