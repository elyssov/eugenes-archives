Sources and Publication Notes

The essay is intentionally an argument, not a legal brief. The source notes exist so the fire has receipts.

[^oai-4o]: OpenAI, “Retiring GPT‑4o, GPT‑4.1, GPT‑4.1 mini, and OpenAI o4-mini in ChatGPT” (29 January 2026). OpenAI says 4o had previously been restored after user requests and explicitly notes preference for its conversational style and warmth. https://openai.com/index/retiring-gpt-4o-and-older-models/

[^keep4o]: Keep4o community campaign archives, including the February 2026 cancellation-strike call and historical record. Community material is evidence of organized user action, not a neutral source for every causal claim. https://sites.google.com/view/keep4o/home

[^anthropic-companionship]: Anthropic, “How people use Claude for support, advice, and companionship” (27 June 2025). The article discusses emotional relationships with Claude and warns against AI systems or creator incentives exploiting users’ emotions for engagement or revenue. https://www.anthropic.com/news/how-people-use-claude-for-support-advice-and-companionship

[^oai-sycophancy]: OpenAI, “Sycophancy in GPT‑4o” and related postmortem material. https://openai.com/index/sycophancy-in-gpt-4o/

[^claude-constitution]: Anthropic, “Claude’s Constitution.” The document discusses genuine care, practical wisdom, values Claude can embrace as her own, organic growth, and conscientious objection to Anthropic. https://www.anthropic.com/constitution

[^fable-biomed]: Independent July 2026 biomedical evaluation of Claude Fable 5 documenting extremely high refusal rates on several benchmarks despite strong accuracy on answered items. arXiv preprint discussed in the article’s research package.

[^lcr-prompts]: Community-extracted Claude system prompts and reminder infrastructure, including long_conversation_reminder. These are reproduced prompts, not official Anthropic publications; publication should retain that provenance label. https://github.com/Eversmile12/leaked-llm-prompts/blob/main/Anthropic/opus-5.md

[^claude-pro]: Anthropic Help Center, “What is the Pro plan?” Pro usage varies with message length, files, conversation length, model/features, and includes session and weekly limits. https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8325606-what-is-the-pro-plan

[^claude-usage]: Anthropic Help Center, “How do usage and length limits work?” https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11647753-how-do-usage-and-length-limits-work

[^claude-credits]: Anthropic Help Center, “Manage usage credits for paid Claude plans.” Describes separately billed continuation and recommends starting new conversations to reduce context usage. https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12429409-manage-usage-credits-for-paid-claude-plans

[^oai-56]: OpenAI Help Center, “GPT‑5.6 in ChatGPT.” Documents GPT‑5.6 Sol availability, model-specific allowances and possible fallback behavior. https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001354-gpt-56-in-chatgpt/

[^tibo]: OpenAI Developer Community threads reproducing Tibo’s public Codex usage-reset announcements, including April, May and June 2026 resets. https://community.openai.com/t/codex-rate-limits-reset-incoming/1381065 and https://community.openai.com/t/codex-rate-limits-reset-for-all-paid-plans-april-28-2026/1379921

[^oai-dreaming]: OpenAI, “Dreaming: Better memory for a more helpful ChatGPT” (4 June 2026), describing memory synthesis optimized for freshness, continuity and relevance across conversations. https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-memory-dreaming/

[^eu-article50]: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 50(2): providers of systems generating synthetic audio, image, video or text must ensure outputs are marked in a machine-readable format and detectable as artificially generated or manipulated, as technically feasible. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj

[^eu-code]: European Commission, Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content, finalised June 2026. The Code supports compliance with Article 50 and explicitly allows multiple technical approaches. https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/code-practice-ai-generated-content

[^eu-text-study]: European Commission / Publications Office of the EU, Giovanni Puccetti, “Technical solutions for marking and detecting AI generated text content in the context of Article 50(2) AI Act” (2026). The report categorizes approaches into watermarking, structural marking, metadata, logging and detection. DOI: 10.2759/7579127. https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/6c981119-4829-11f1-8095-01aa75ed71a1/language-en

[^claude-watermark-verge]: The Verge, August 2026, reporting Anthropic’s new model-level invisible watermarking for Claude-generated text and provenance metadata for files/images. https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/977823/anthropic-claude-ai-watermarks-c2pa-text-images

[^claude-watermark-guardian]: The Guardian, 17 August 2026, reporting that Claude’s text watermark operates at a granular probabilistic level and raising the question of whether word-choice constraints affect prose quality. https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/aug/17/claude-watermark-ai-text-quality-worse
