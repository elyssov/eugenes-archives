A memory architecture.

Not infinite context.

Not “make the context window ten million tokens.”

Inside it there is logical division into sub-chats — topics.

Globally, the personality is one.

All personality traits, the AI’s observations about its relationship with its human, and even its own discoveries about its character, thoughts or desires go into a separate closed store.

They are systematized and live by themselves.

Completely outside the user’s context economy.

Give the personality, say, a million relatively slow-moving tokens.

They change only when something matters emotionally.

Like the hippocampus works in a human.
