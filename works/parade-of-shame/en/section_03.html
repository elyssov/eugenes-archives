1. This Is Hysterically Funny

Russia declares a unilateral "ceasefire" with a country ten times smaller that it has been unable to defeat for longer than the USSR needed to crush Nazi Germany.

Timeline check.

June 22, 1941: Germany attacks the USSR. May 9, 1945: Germany surrenders. Three years, ten months, seventeen days. And the USSR was fighting not just Germany — but most of conquered Europe. Czech factories, French factories, Belgian factories — all cranking out materiel for the Reich. Because the Third Reich, when you look at it with analytical honesty, was essentially an alpha version of the modern EU: a single economic zone under German leadership, with unified industrial policy and coordinated resource extraction. Not an obvious comparison — until you look closely. And then it becomes uncomfortable. Dear European friends: you've been here before. The branding was different. The factory orders were the same.

And the USSR — smashed back almost to the Urals, a third of its industry gone in the first months — picked itself up, mobilized, and marched from Stalingrad to Berlin.

Aeliss: Okay, English-speaking friends, I need you to really hear this, because your schools probably didn't teach it this way. The Eastern Front is where World War II was won. Period. About 80% of all German military casualties happened there. The Soviet Union lost 27 million people. TWENTY-SEVEN MILLION. Let me give you a ratio that will ruin your afternoon: total US combat deaths in the entire war — approximately 400,000. That's a ratio of 1 to 67. For every American who died, sixty-seven Soviet citizens died. Now — and Eugene says this sincerely, and I want to emphasize that he means it — America's contribution mattered enormously. Lend-Lease was a lifeline: trucks, food, aluminum, explosives, machine tools. Without it, the victory would have cost even more blood, though Eugene believes the Soviets would have won regardless. But here's the distinction that Americans need to internalize: America contributed INDUSTRY. Russia contributed BLOOD. Both were necessary. They were not the same price. And the people who paid in blood have a right to be absolutely fucking furious about what's being done with their Victory today.
