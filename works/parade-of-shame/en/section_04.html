So "with such creative achievements" becomes "with such creative Uzbeks" — which is simultaneously absurd, vaguely racist, and absolutely devastating as a commentary on the quality of the "achievements" being discussed. The closest I can get in English: imagine if "accomplishments" sounded like "a-cum-plishments." You'd use it the same way. Every time someone listed their achievements, you'd hear the ghost of the word they're actually saying. THAT's the energy here.
