3. Begging the Enemy for a Timeout

The attempt to wheedle security guarantees from the enemy — for this event that, in Putin's case, is literally blasphemous — is pathetic beyond anything I can describe without resorting to medical terminology.

Stalin, in 1941, held a military parade during the Battle of Moscow. Units marched across Red Square and went directly from the cobblestones to the front lines.

And they won.

Now Putin's regime declares ceasefires and begs Ukraine not to hurt them. At least on May 9th. While shutting down the internet across Moscow from May 6th through the 10th, because the evil Ukrainians might use Moscow's own cell towers and public Wi-Fi to guide drones to the parade.

I'm trying to imagine Stalin writing to Hitler in November 1941, asking him to please pause the offensive and let Moscow hold a parade.

Can't do it.
