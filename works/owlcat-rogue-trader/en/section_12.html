WARHAMMER IS NOT "MORE SKULLS"

I want to return to those skulls for a moment.

And the statue.

Because this is a larger disease in licensed art.

People learn surface grammar.

Warhammer has skulls.

Warhammer has candles.

Warhammer has Gothic arches.

Warhammer has giant gears.

Warhammer has cathedral ships.

Therefore:

Skull + candle + arch + gear + pipe = Warhammer.

No.

That is how you get theme-park Warhammer.

Real design language asks what those objects mean.

The skull can mean mortality, office, martyrdom, authority, ancestry, holy relic, machine interface.

The candle can mean ritual, light, prayer, maintenance, mourning.

The gear can be iconography or mechanism.

The cathedral geometry can support class hierarchy, religion, acoustics, defense, procession.

The same visual object can do multiple jobs.

That is why the setting survives its absurdity.

It is not random absurdity.

It is institutional absurdity.

A bureaucracy became a religion and survived for ten thousand years.

A navy inherited technology it barely understands and still operates it because the procedures became sacred.

A priest oils a weapon while chanting because the chant is simultaneously superstition, checklist, and culture.

That is rich.

A random giant gear under the bridge because "Mechanicus" is not rich.

It is clip art.

And the immortal sword skeleton who apparently emigrates from shrine to shrine is not iconography.

He is the art pipeline waving at you.
