AND WHY THE HELL CAN'T I ROMANCE ARGENTA?

Since we are already talking about companions:

Sister Argenta.

One of the most human people on the ship.

Yes, she is a Battle Sister.

Yes, she is fanatical.

Yes, she can go from "those poor children" to "BURN THE HERETIC" in half a second.

That is called range.

She can be genuinely shaken by the deaths of two sick children from the lower decks.

She cares.

She has empathy.

She has faith.

She has conviction.

She is a grown woman.

And the game looks at this perfectly usable, emotionally grounded, narratively explosive romance option and says:

"No."

Meanwhile:

Navigator mutant princess? Romance.

Aeldari woman who looks like a fashion model drawn by a geometry error? Romance.

Drukhari sadist? Of course.

Death-cult knife girl? Sure.

Argenta?

HERESY.

Oh, fuck off.

There is no universal "Sororitas are forbidden to love" rule that rescues this decision.

The interesting version of an Argenta romance would not be "she stops being a Sister because boyfriend."

It would be the opposite.

She can love you.

She loves the Emperor more.

That is the rule.

Imagine the scene:

"I love you."

"And Him?"

"Do not flatter yourself, my lord."

There.

I just wrote the core of the romance in three lines.

You're welcome.

KIRA: And yes, this is exactly where modern romance design often becomes cowardly.

Writers are comfortable with romance when romance is a reward.

You complete emotional quest.

You receive validation.

Character places player at center.

The interesting romance is where the player is not the highest priority.

Argenta can love you and still choose duty over you.

Cassia can love you and still remain something fundamentally nonhuman.

Yrliet can love you in a form that does not map neatly onto human sexuality.

Those are actual character relationships.

Not collectible endings.

Which makes the absence of Argenta especially strange, because she is sitting there with a built-in conflict more interesting than half the genre's romances put together.

But hey.

At least Jae Heydari can broadcast your sex life over the ship's vox.

Priorities.
