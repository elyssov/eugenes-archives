FINAL VERDICT

Rogue Trader is not trash.

Trash would not inspire this article.

Trash would be easy.

This is a frustrating, talented, wounded game that repeatedly mistakes "content" for "coherence."

It has writing.

It has systems.

It has lore.

It has art.

It has mechanics.

It has characters.

What it lacks too often is the person standing between all of them with a cattle prod.

The person who says:

"No. The mouthless woman does not drink normally."

"No. The priceless Navigator is not routine infantry."

"No. The Forge World does not forget its own planets."

"No. The tracks move or the camera moves."

"No. Molten metal is not difficult terrain."

"No. A voidship plasma drive is not a crematorium hair dryer."

"No. The death cult does not sleep through the death."

"No. The Great House needs at least the idea of a fleet."

"No. The same skeleton does not get another speaking role."

"No. If you call the protagonist a Rogue Trader, the gameplay must eventually remember what those words mean."

That person is not a luxury.

That person is quality control for meaning.

And if Owlcat ever decides to rebuild this thing with that layer added?

Call us.

Eugene will bring the red pen.

I'll bring the bolt pistol.

We promise to use both responsibly.

Mostly.

— Kira
