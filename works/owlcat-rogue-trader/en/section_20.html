CASSIA: THE TEXT SAYS SLENDER WOMAN; THE ART SAYS TALL GIRL

And now the other Cassia problem.

I need to correct something from the earlier version of this article because after staring at the art, the model, and the actual text side by side, the mismatch is sharper than I first described it.

The text wants Cassia to feel alien.

Not "unusual."

Alien.

It emphasizes unnaturally long limbs, talon-like hands, gill structures, a body that is supposed to make the reader register:

"This is shaped like a woman, but something has gone very wrong with the template."

And then, in the same game, you can romance her.

Excuse me?

If I take the prose absolutely literally, my first romantic thought should not be:

"What a mysterious young lady."

It should be:

"Why is the female Slender Man wearing court dress, and has anyone informed the Ordo?"

The prose does not describe "beautiful but exotic."

It describes foreignness as anatomy.

That matters.

Because romance begins with the player being willing to perceive the character as a possible romantic object in the first place.

You can absolutely write a romance with a genuinely alien creature.

Fine.

But then OWN IT.

Make that the point.

Do not write "unnaturally elongated predatory mutant" and then draw "very tall pale girl who needs a manicure."

Because the art does not support the horror.

The official art shows a young woman who is strange, yes.

Pale.

Red-eyed.

Third eye.

Very tall.

Long fingers.

But "alien horror"?

No.

She is cute.

She is unusual.

She looks like a roughly two-meter-tall aristocratic girl with Navigator features.

On the in-game model she stands about a head above my Rogue Trader and Abelard Werserian — roughly in the same tall-person territory as Yrliet, though the exact proportions differ.

She does not look like somebody took human anatomy and dragged the scale sliders until the rig filed a complaint.

She looks like a really fucking tall woman.

That is a different design.

And honestly?

It is the better one.
