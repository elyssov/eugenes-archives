LET THE PLAYER DELEGATE

And this is where the correct Rogue Trader verb becomes obvious. If anyone ever remakes this:

Give me the most aristocratic button in RPG history.

DELEGATE.

Bandits?

Delegate.

Minor cult?

Delegate.

Cargo dispute?

Delegate.

Routine arrest?

Delegate.

Then show the result.

Sometimes my officer succeeds.

Sometimes fails.

Sometimes steals.

Sometimes overreacts.

Sometimes I get a new quest because the man I trusted created a catastrophe.

Now delegation is not "skip content."

It is rule through people.

That is how power becomes gameplay.

The more authority you have, the less your game should be about doing everything yourself — and the more it should be about choosing who acts in your name.

That is a Rogue Trader mechanic.

Not another +5% talent.
