HOW IT SHOULD HAVE WORKED: THREE LEVELS OF POWER

The game should have had three scales.

1. GOVERNANCE

You are in the strategium.

You decide:

  • which regiment goes where;
  • which noble gets the contract;
  • which colony receives scarce machinery;
  • whether the Mechanicus gets excavation rights;
  • which governor remains in office;
  • which trade route gets protection;
  • whether a suspected cult is infiltrated, quarantined, or burned out;
  • whether the Navy gets support;
  • whether you tolerate a xenos contact.

This can be mostly text, maps, portraits, and numbers.

Cheap by AAA standards.

Incredibly appropriate for Rogue Trader.

2. OPERATION

You personally arrive with actual troops.

The map contains friendly armsmen, officers, servitors, maybe a squad of elite household troops.

The big war is happening around you.

You are not clearing the factory yourself.

You are solving the important problem inside the factory while your people hold corridors, secure lifts, breach bulkheads, and die in the background.

Now the scale makes sense.

3. PERSONAL ACTION

Here comes the classic six-person party.

Secret meeting.

Xenos ruin.

Diplomatic encounter where military presence would ruin everything.

Archaeotech site.

Warp anomaly.

Political assassination attempt.

A chamber that only six people can reach.

Something where the personal presence of the Warrant bearer is the point.

Now the moment you step onto the ground, it matters.

People should react:

"The Rogue Trader came personally."

That sentence should carry weight.

Instead the current game teaches everyone in the Expanse:

"If you leave a quest marker in a basement, the head of House von Valancius will eventually come downstairs alone."

That is not a statesman.

That is DoorDash with a plasma pistol.
