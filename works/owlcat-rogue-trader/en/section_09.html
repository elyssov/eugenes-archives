TWO "UNEXPLORED" PLANETS IN MY OWN FUCKING SYSTEM

And here is where the scale problem becomes comedy.

You arrive in a system belonging to your own House.

There is a Forge World there.

An actual Forge World.

Massive industry.

Shipping.

Mechanicus infrastructure.

Centuries of surveys, routes, orbital calculations, mining interests, defense planning, sensor coverage.

And there are planets marked effectively as unexplored.

In your system.

Your House's system.

With a Forge World.

Owlcat.

Come here.

I just want to talk.

No, put the helmet down.

KIRA: This is "the world only exists when the player clicks on it" design at its purest.

An unexploited planet?

Fine.

An insufficiently surveyed surface?

Fine.

Restricted Mechanicus data?

Great.

Lost archives?

Excellent.

A Warp phenomenon that recently changed the system?

Perfect.

But a planet in a long-held, economically active system being treated like Columbus just found it behind the sofa?

No.

Your dynasty may not know whether Valley 8-B contains promethium deposits.

It knows the planet exists.

A Forge World would know the mass, orbit, resource potential, magnetic field, likely mining value, possible defense use, moons, and probably the name of the servitor whose great-grandprocessor first catalogued it.

This is the Mechanicus.

They can be ignorant in fascinating ways.

They are not unable to look up.
