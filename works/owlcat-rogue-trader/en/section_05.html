WHERE IS MY FUCKING ARMORY?

Now we reach the loot economy.

This is Theodora's personal ship.

Theodora is the head of an ancient Rogue Trader dynasty.

Where is her armory?

Not the emergency rack.

Not the crew lasguns.

Not the box where Deck Security keeps three grenades and a disciplinary baton.

The personal armory.

Where are the master-crafted bolt pistols?

Plasma pistols?

Melta weapons?

Power blades?

Carapace armour?

Power armour?

Personal force fields?

Archaeotech?

Digital weapons?

Jokaero nonsense?

The sort of beautiful, dangerous, probably-questionable object that makes an Inquisitor raise one eyebrow and a Tech-Priest whisper "mine"?

The master-at-arms had a melta.

Where is it?

Did Theodora lose it in a card game?

Was the Great House running a pawn shop?

Did she spend the dynasty's entire procurement budget on robed skeleton statues?

Because by Act II I, the player, have assembled a better personal arsenal through random adventuring than the supposedly ancient interstellar aristocracy had available on its flagship.

That is lunacy.

KIRA: Classic RPG progression starts you poor and makes you rich.

Rogue Trader progression should start you rich and make you obscene.

That is the entire fix.

Starter pistol?

Master-crafted bolter.

Midgame pistol?

Rare plasma weapon.

Late game?

A pre-Heresy relic with a provenance longer than the ship's crew manifest.

Starter armour?

Excellent carapace armour or a dynasty field generator.

Midgame?

Human-scale power armour.

Late game?

Artificer-level monstrosity that requires a separate diplomatic apology to the Mechanicus.

Starter melee?

Power sword.

Late game?

Something whose legal status changes depending on which Inquisitor is standing closest.

You can preserve the exact psychological rhythm of loot progression.

"Number goes up."

Players happy.

But now the numbers go from aristocratic privilege to interstellar obscenity, not from "found a better vest in a trash pile" to "finally dressed like my job title."

And a nerd correction while I'm here: a Rosarius is the Ecclesiarchal personal field device people often think of in this context; an Inquisitorial rosette is the badge/credential. Rogue Traders can absolutely possess various refractor, conversion, archeotech, and other personal field systems, but let us not accidentally give Theodora an Archbishop's jewelry box.

See?

This is how correction should work.

Not a legal disclaimer.

A knife, a joke, and onward.
