Transports.

Patrol vessels.

Couriers.

Ore haulers.

Chartered merchantmen.

Armed escorts.

Mechanicus traffic around the Forge World.

Pilgrim craft.

Something.

Lovely.

A great interstellar House whose visible spaceborne power consists of one frigate is not "mysterious."

It is an LLC with a dramatic logo.

Put named allied ships on the map.

Mention them.

Let one appear in the background of a battle.

"The transport Saint Drusus' Patience is overdue."

Give an escort a name.

Let it survive three chapters.
