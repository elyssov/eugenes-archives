SPACE COMBAT: BATTLEFLEET GOTHIC FROM TEMU

Then there is void combat.

Others have complained about this at length, so I will not spend another twenty pages on it.

But I will say one thing:

A Rogue Trader game should make a broadside feel like a geopolitical statement.

Imperial void combat is about enormous, slow, cathedral-shaped ships committing industrial-scale violence.

Macrocannons are not little pew-pew turrets.

A broadside is a wall of mass and explosive force.

The ships should feel like cities trying to murder each other.

Instead the scale often reads strangely, escort classes can look wildly mismatched, and the whole thing sometimes feels like Battlefleet Gothic after being retold to someone over a bad phone connection.

I chose the Firestorm because I prefer direct gunfire to babysitting torpedoes.

And yes, it works.

But again:

Why is the lord of a great House personally operating at the scale of a frigate duel with no meaningful dynastic fleet around him?

Same disease.

Different screen.
