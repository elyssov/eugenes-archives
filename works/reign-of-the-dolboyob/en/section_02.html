All in the same direction. All producing the same output: systems run by people who are too incompetent to understand their own incompetence.

This is not a conspiracy. Conspiracies require intelligence.

This is the Reign of the Dolboyob.

I'm going to prove it with examples. Not sorted by category — scattered deliberately. Because when you see it sorted, you think "that industry has problems." When you see it scattered, you see the pattern: a single disease, metastasizing across every system it touches.
