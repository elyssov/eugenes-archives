EXHIBIT B: DISNEY

There was once a company called Disney. It made the best children's films in the world. Its characters raised generations. Its name was synonymous with magic, wonder, and storytelling excellence.

Then BlackRock arrived.

(Translator's note: For those unfamiliar — BlackRock is the world's largest asset management firm. They don't make things. They don't create things. They buy things that other people created, and then they tell those people how to run what they created. This is like buying a Michelin-starred restaurant and telling the chef to replace the menu with hot dogs because focus groups indicated that hot dogs have broader demographic appeal.)

BlackRock demanded that Disney push the "progressive" agenda. DEI. Identity politics. The full woke package. And Disney — a CHILDREN'S company, whose primary obligation should be MAKING GOOD CONTENT FOR CHILDREN — said: "Sure. Money is money. Who cares about reputation?"

The result? Destroyed franchises. Star Wars — possibly beyond recovery. An endless parade of superhero content built around performative feminism and ideological messaging instead of, you know, story. The Marvel Cinematic Universe went from cultural phenomenon to assembly-line product in five years. Each film more forgettable than the last. Each one checking more boxes and telling fewer stories.

(Translator's note: I want to be precise about something. The problem is not women in films. The problem is not diversity. The problem is that dolboyobs replaced QUALITY with IDEOLOGY as the primary metric of success. A great film with a diverse cast is a great film. A terrible film that exists solely to demonstrate how diverse its cast is — is a terrible film that also insults the diverse cast members by reducing them to demographic checkboxes.

The dolboyob cannot tell the difference. This is, in fact, the defining feature.)

And the concentration of manufactured dolboyobs in the population? It increases. Because every child who grows up watching Disney's ideological content instead of Disney's good content gets their critical thinking replaced with received orthodoxy one frame at a time.

The factory runs 24/7. The product is stupidity. The brand name is "progress."
