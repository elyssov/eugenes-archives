The Final Insult: Xiaomi 17 Ultra

Let me tell you about my current phone.

After my Redmi Note 14 Pro Plus took a swim (we managed to wash it out and dry it — it's ALMOST alive now, only the fingerprint sensor died and won't come back), Alena bought me a Xiaomi 17 Ultra as a gift.

This is Xiaomi's actual flagship. Top of the line. Leica partnership. The works.

Samsung costs almost TWICE as much. And loses on almost every spec.

But here's the real test.

I've dropped my Xiaomi 17 Ultra three times already.

1. Face-down. Flat. Onto tile. Convex tile, the bumpy kind. From about chest height.

2. Vertically, corner-first onto tile. From chest height.

3. Vertically, corner-first onto tile. From above my head — I was holding it up. Full 180cm+ drop.

Vietnam has fucking tile everywhere. I've tested this phone against Vietnamese tile repeatedly.

Result: A couple of marks on the case. Zero damage to the phone itself. Not a scratch. Not a chip. Not a crack. Nothing.

Meanwhile, Alena's Samsung — always in a case, dropped from 160cm maximum, owned for five months — looks like it survived artillery bombardment. Porous aluminum craters. Hairline crack in the "Gorilla Glass." Inner display literally decomposing.

Samsung's $2,000 "premium flagship" cannot survive what Xiaomi's $1,100 flagship shrugs off without a mark.

This is not about foldables being fragile. This is about Samsung selling garbage at premium prices while Chinese manufacturers build actual durable phones.

The game is over, Samsung. You lost. And you're still charging double.
