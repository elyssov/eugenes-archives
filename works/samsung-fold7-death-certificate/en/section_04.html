The Waiting Room From Hell

Here's where it gets interesting.

While we sat there in that service center, sweating in the Nha Trang noon heat, waiting for our verdict — two more customers walked in.

With Fold devices.

In half an hour.

A third Fold was already on the operating table, waiting for an inner screen replacement. The technician even showed us a replacement display as an example. For Alena's phone, they'd have to order another one — because the available parts had already been used up.

Let me repeat that.

Tiny city. Midday heat. Remote service center. Half an hour.

Three Fold casualties. Plus ours makes four.

This is not a coincidence. This is not "one unlucky customer." This is the smell of a commercial catastrophe.

When your premium foldables appear in a small-town service center like wounded soldiers at a field hospital, you don't hide behind scratches on a frame. You investigate. You escalate. You protect the customer before the customer publicly destroys your reputation.

Samsung chose the frame scratches.
